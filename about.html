Built to protect a working port.

Since 1948, PFPD has served the waterfront, waterways, workers, residents, and visitors of Florence.

Our department

Our mission

To protect the people, waterways, and commerce of Florence with courage, fairness, and an unwavering commitment to public trust.

Our jurisdiction

PFPD provides law enforcement and emergency response across port terminals, public waterfronts, navigable waterways, and critical transportation facilities throughout the Florence Harbor District.

Our values

Integrity guides every decision. Service puts our community first. Vigilance keeps our port prepared. Respect shapes every interaction with the people we serve.
